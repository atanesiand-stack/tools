How to price a window cleaning job

A flat price per pane is quick to quote, but it hides the things that decide whether a job pays: second-story work, hard water, screens and tracks, and the drive to get there. Pricing from time keeps those visible. Count what is on the house, multiply by how long each item takes you, and let your costs and margin set the price.

  1. Walk the property and count panes by type: standard, picture windows, French panes, sliding doors, skylights.
  2. Count the extras that take real time: screens, tracks and sills.
  3. Mark anything slower than normal, such as second-story glass, hard water stains or post-construction cleanup.
  4. Add travel and setup time. On small jobs it can be a quarter of the day.
  5. Turn hours into cost: wages, payroll burden, supplies, overhead.
  6. Divide the cost by one minus your profit margin, and never go below your minimum job charge.

Which numbers should you use?

The minutes per pane in this calculator are examples so you can see it working. They are not an industry standard. Time yourself on two or three real jobs, divide the minutes by the panes, and type your own numbers into the minutes column. Your speed with a squeegee, a water-fed pole or a ladder will not match anyone else's.

What the result tells you

  • Break-even cost is what the job costs you with no profit.
  • Job price is the break-even cost plus your margin, rounded to the nearest $5, and never below your minimum.
  • Price per item lets you compare your time-based price with a per-pane price list.
  • Revenue per labor hour shows whether the job is worth the drive.

Questions

Is this calculator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser, there is no sign-up, and the numbers you enter are not sent anywhere.

Should I charge per pane or per hour?

Customers usually prefer a fixed price, so quote a fixed price. Use time to work out what that fixed price needs to be.

What is a minimum job charge?

It is the lowest price you accept for showing up. Small jobs still cost you travel and setup time, so the calculator never returns less than your minimum.

How do I price repeat customers?

Enter the visits per year. The calculator shows the yearly and average monthly value of the customer at the job price.
