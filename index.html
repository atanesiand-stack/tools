Why price from time instead of a flat rate?

A flat rate copied from a competitor does not know what you pay your crew, how far you drive, or how long the job really takes. Each calculator here works the same way: hours first, then wages, payroll burden, supplies and overhead, then the profit margin you choose. The result is a price you can defend and a break-even number you never go below.
